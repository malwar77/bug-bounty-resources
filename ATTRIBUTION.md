## 2. External Resource Usage & Permission Audit

This repository operates strictly as a **curated index, directory, and pedagogical reference list**. It adheres to informational fair use principles:

| Resource Type | Scope & Nature of Inclusion | Permission & Copyright Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| **Commercial Books & Guides** (e.g., No Starch Press, Wiley, Amazon links) | Titles, author names, descriptions, and publisher/retail links. | **Compliant**: Bibliographic references and hyperlinks to commercial works do not copy protected expressive text or infringe copyright. |
| **Open Source Tools** (e.g., ProjectDiscovery, OWASP, ffuf, Caido, Burp extensions) | Tool names, short metadata summaries, installation prerequisites, and repository hyperlinks. | **Compliant**: Standard fair-use descriptive reference. All tools remain under their respective upstream licenses (e.g., MIT, Apache 2.0, GPL, or proprietary). No third-party binaries or proprietary code are bundled in this repo. |
| **Community Blog Posts & Writeups** (Medium, personal blogs, HackerOne disclosure links) | Titles, author attribution, and hyperlinks. | **Compliant**: Pure link aggregation with explicit attribution to the original researchers. No full-text scraping or redistribution of articles. |
| **Online Labs & Training Platforms** (PortSwigger Web Security Academy, Hack The Box, TryHackMe) | Platform names, descriptions, and official entry URLs. | **Compliant**: Descriptive references to public training services. |
| **Community Video & Audio Media** (YouTube talks, podcasts, Twitch streams) | Channel names, presentation titles, and platform URLs. | **Compliant**: Hyperlinks to officially published public multimedia. |
